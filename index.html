03

Smart Smoke Detector with Event Counter and Audible Alarm for Community Fire Alerts

A smoke detection unit that logs trigger events, sounds a community-grade audible alarm, and reports cumulative event counts.

My Contributions

Led a five-member team, with primary responsibility for sensor selection, microcontroller firmware development, event-counter logging, alarm circuitry, and enclosure design.

Results Detected smoke at 300 ppm threshold in under 4 seconds, correctly logged 100% of simulated trigger events across 50 test cycles.

Remote Control Pesticide Machine

A wirelessly operated pesticide sprayer built for agricultural use, reducing direct human exposure to chemicals. Bluetooth-controlled device designed for field terrain.

My Contributions

Worked closely with the client as a commissioned developer, focusing on reliability and usability to translate their needs into a functional prototype. Particularly, creating circuit and block diagrams, schematic design, motor driver integration, pump control circuit, overall wiring, and range testing.

Results Reliable control at 35m range, reduced operator pesticide exposure by eliminating direct handling during spraying operations.

My Contributions

06

Web-Enabled Voice-Activated 6-Axis Multimodal Robotic Arm with Distributed SBC

A distributed SBC-based system that drives a full 6-axis robotic arm through both a web dashboard and real-time voice commands. Multimodal control architecture processes speech, manual web inputs, and sensor data simultaneously across Lattepanda V1.

My Contributions

Led a five-member team, ensuring progress by stepping in to cover responsibilities when teammates were unavailable. My primary focus was on hardware procurement, circuit design, and system assembly, while also supporting other areas as needed to keep the project on track.

Results Voice-to-motion latency under 1500 ms, successfully executed distinct voice commands with 90% recognition accuracy in a noisy lab environment.
